# Copy rules

Read before writing any text that isn't already in `content/`: alt text, aria-labels,
meta and Open Graph tags, button and link labels, nav labels, breadcrumbs, the 404 page,
image captions, footer text, and anything inside JSON-LD.

Approved copy in `content/` is not subject to these rules — it has already passed them.
Don't "improve" it.

## Default: write less

If a label can reuse approved words, reuse them. If an element needs no text, give it none.
Decorative images get `alt=""`. When in doubt, add a `[NEEDS:]` rather than composing a line.

## Voice, in one line

He explains, and the explanation is what reassures. A comforting line must be a fact about
what happens, never an assertion that things will be fine. UK spelling. Keep English
treatment terms ("root canal", "braces", "scaling").

## Never say, anywhere

- **painless, pain-free, won't hurt, gentle, comfortable** as a claim. Say what happens
  instead: gel or spray on the gum before the injection, antibiotics and painkillers after.
- **guaranteed, 100%, permanent, lifetime, forever**
- **best, leading, top, number one, premier, trusted** — no superlative in the clinic's voice
- **orthodontist, specialist, consultant, expert** for Dr Abaid
- **state-of-the-art, world-class, cutting-edge, advanced technology, modern equipment**
- **your smile journey, we care about your smile, smile transformation, brighter smile**
- **any success rate or outcome percentage**

## Never mention

- **Implants** or **veneers**, in any form.
- **Star ratings, review counts**, "rated X", "over N reviews". One exception, approved
  by Uzair on 24 Sep 2026: the clinic's overall **Google rating, ★ 4.7/5**, on the Dr Abaid
  card. It is never shown next to a review, never given a review count, and never put in the
  JSON-LD.
- **The drilling noise**, a **stop signal**, **headphones**, **warranty exclusions**,
  **certificates on display**, **which side of the road** the clinic is on, the
  **clinic email address**. Each was decided off the site.
- **A team.** There are no staff photos or staff facts; the site speaks only about Dr Abaid.

## Quoted exceptions

Three reviews contain words banned in the clinic's voice ("Best Dental facility in
Faisalabad", "state-of-the-art"). They stay **inside the quote** and are never lifted into
a heading, pull-quote, alt text, meta description or highlight. Faris Z.'s rating is never shown.

"Very minimal" (post-operative pain) is Dr Abaid's phrase and appears in approved copy.
Don't reuse it in new text.

## Prices

Treatment prices are always "from" figures — no ranges, no upper bounds.
The consultation fee, PKR 300, is the one flat figure. Don't write new price text at all;
reuse the approved lines.

## Equipment

Never named alone. The intraoral camera, digital X-rays, rotary instruments and the
ultrasonic scaler only appear alongside what the patient gets from them — and only where
approved copy already names them.

## Images and alt text

Alt text says what is in the photograph and nothing more: "Dr Abaid Khalil",
"Treatment room at Abaid Dental Care", "Abaid Dental Care from the street".
No treatment claims, no service keywords. Before/after alt text names the treatment
shown and says before or after — nothing about the result.

## Children

Braces start from **age 7**. Never 9.

## Missing facts

Write `[NEEDS: the specific fact]`. Never fill a gap with something plausible, and never
soften a gap into a vague line that reads as finished.
