## What happens
@id: process
@visual: interior

An ultrasonic scaler clears away the plaque and tartar built up around and between your teeth.

The surfaces are then polished, which smooths them and lifts surface stains.

A routine cleaning takes 20 to 30 minutes. A little longer if it is your first visit, or if there is heavier build-up to clear.

## Does it damage the enamel?

@visual: none

No.

Scaling removes plaque and tartar that are sitting **on top of** the enamel. The enamel itself is not what is being taken off.

Teeth can feel sensitive for a few days afterwards. That is normal and it settles.
