## Whitening is not a cleaning
@id: not-a-cleaning
@link: /teeth-cleaning | Read about teeth cleaning
@visual: none
@note: Approved to ship with these two paragraphs. A third paragraph is waiting on Dr Abaid (when a cleaning is enough and when whitening is right); when it arrives it goes after the second paragraph.

A teeth cleaning takes off plaque and tartar, and the polishing afterwards lifts stains sitting on the surface. For some people that is enough, and it costs PKR 4,500.

Whitening is the separate treatment, and it costs more.

## Two ways to do it
@id: options
@visual: interior

**In the clinic.** One visit, 45 minutes to an hour.

**At home.** Trays made for your teeth, worn daily over one to two weeks.

Dr Abaid will tell you which suits you when he has looked.

## How long it lasts
@id: longevity
@visual: none

One to two years, for most people.

What shortens it is what you drink and whether you smoke. Coffee and tea stain teeth again over time, and so does smoking.

## Is it safe for the teeth?
@id: safety
@cta: whatsapp
@visual: none

Yes, done under professional supervision. The enamel is not damaged by it.

What does carry a risk is over-using the kits sold over the counter. The treatment is not the problem; using too much of it without anyone checking is.
