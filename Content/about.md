## He'll explain before treatment starts
@id: how-he-works
@link: /first-visit | More about what happens on a first visit
@visual: interior
@note: Use an interior image where the screen is visible, if one exists.

He tells you what is wrong with the tooth, what he plans to do about it, and what it will cost. The final cost is confirmed once he has looked at the tooth, not before.

If you want to see what he is looking at, the camera puts your own tooth on the screen beside you while he talks through it.

Ask him how a root canal works and he will take you through it in order: the anaesthetic gel that goes on before the injection, opening the tooth, clearing and irrigating the canal, filling it, and the final filling on top. Usually one to four visits, each around half an hour.

No. He is a dentist who provides orthodontic treatment, and he holds C.Orth, a certificate in orthodontics.

### Does he treat children?

Yes, children and adults. Braces start from age 7.

### How long has he been a dentist?

Six years, all of them at this clinic. Around 3,000 patients have been treated in that time.

### What happens on a first visit?

Anaesthetic gel or spray goes on the gum before any injection, and you are given antibiotics and painkillers afterwards. The first-visit page goes through it properly.

We reply on WhatsApp within one to two hours.
