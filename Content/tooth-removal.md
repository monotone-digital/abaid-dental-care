## Simple or surgical
@id: simple-or-surgical
@visual: none
@note: Approved to ship. Awaiting Dr Abaid's own description; if it arrives, this body is replaced, not appended to.

A simple removal is a tooth that has come through normally and can be lifted out.

A surgical removal is for a tooth that has not come through fully, or has broken at the gum line. It takes a little more work to reach.

Dr Abaid will tell you which one yours is before he starts, and what that means for the appointment.

## What happens on the day
@id: on-the-day
@visual: none

Anaesthetic gel or spray goes on the gum first, so you do not feel the injection. You are anaesthetised before anything else happens.

Once the tooth is out, a gauze pack goes over the socket and you bite down on it.

Afterwards you are given antibiotics or painkillers, depending on the case.

## What to do afterwards
@id: aftercare
@sub: Dr Abaid gives these to every patient. They are here so you can read them before you decide.
@visual: none
@note: The most important section on this page. Give it room: each instruction on its own line, readable at arm's length. Not collapsed, not a download.

**Bite on the gauze for about half an hour.** That is what lets the clot form over the socket.

**No spitting and no gargling for the first 24 hours.** Both can dislodge the clot.

**Soft food for at least a day.**

**Chew on the other side for five to seven days.**

**Take the antibiotics or painkillers you are given**, as Dr Abaid tells you.

## Bleeding, swelling and infection

@cta: whatsapp
@visual: none

These are the three things patients ask about most after a tooth comes out.

**Bleeding.** Biting on the gauze for about half an hour is what stops it, by letting a clot form over the socket. Not spitting or gargling for the first day is what keeps that clot in place.

**Infection.** You are given antibiotics where the case calls for them, and the aftercare above is what protects the socket while it closes.

**Pain and swelling.** You are given painkillers, and you are told what to take and when.
