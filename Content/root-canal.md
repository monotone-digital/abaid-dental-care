## Who needs a root canal
@id: who
@visual: none

When decay or damage reaches the nerve inside a tooth, the tooth becomes infected. That is usually what is behind a toothache that will not settle.

At that point there are two options. The tooth comes out, or the inside of it is cleared and the tooth stays.

A root canal is the second one.

## What happens to your tooth
@id: process
@sub: Start to finish, in the order it happens.
@visual: interior
@note: Keep every step as its own paragraph. Never compress into fewer steps — the length is the point.

First, anaesthetic gel or spray goes on the gum, so you do not feel the injection.

Once you are numb, the tooth is opened to reach the pulp chamber, which is where the nerves are.

The blood vessels and nerve tissue are taken out of the chamber, and the inside of the tooth is irrigated.

When all the dead tissue is out, the canal is filled. That step is called obturation.

Then the tooth gets its final filling on top.

Afterwards you are given antibiotics and painkillers, so post-operative pain is very minimal.
