## What the starting price means here
@id: starting-price
@visual: none
@note: This section must sit before the price table, not after it.

Every price below is a starting price. That is what "from" means in the table.

What a treatment actually costs depends on the tooth: how much of it is left, which tooth it is, and which materials the job needs. A cap on a back tooth and a cap on a front tooth are not the same piece of work.

So Dr Abaid looks first, tells you what he has found, and tells you the cost before anything is done.

## How the final cost is decided
@id: final-cost
@link: /first-visit | Read about your first visit
@visual: none

He looks at the tooth. If an X-ray is needed, one is taken.

Then he tells you what he has found, what the options are, and what each one costs.

You decide after that, not before. Simple treatment can often be done the same day if you want it. Anything bigger is booked for its own visit.

## Questions you might have
@id: faq
@type: faq

### Why is every price a "from" price?

Because what a treatment costs depends on the tooth. How much of it is left, which tooth it is, and what materials the job needs all change the figure. The starting price is what the treatment begins at, and Dr Abaid confirms the cost after he has looked.

### When will I know what it actually costs?

At the consultation, before any treatment starts. He tells you what he has found and what each option costs, and you decide after that.

### What payment do you take?

Cash and bank transfer.

### Can I pay in instalments?

Braces and clear aligners can both be paid in stages, on the same terms: around PKR 10,000 for the cast analysis before anything starts, then 30 to 40 per cent in the first month as treatment starts, then PKR 5,000 a month.

### What does the consultation cost?

PKR 300, whatever you are coming in about.

### How quickly will you reply?

We reply on WhatsApp within one to two hours.
