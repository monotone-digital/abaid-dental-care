## What happens when you come in
@id: first-visit
@link: /first-visit | What to expect on your first visit
@visual: treatment

Doctor will have a look at your tooth. If an X-ray is needed, one is taken. Then you're told what has been found and what your options are.

Simple treatment can often be done the same day, a filling or a teeth cleaning, if you want it. Anything bigger is booked for its own visit.

Most people are most nervous about the injection. Anaesthetic gel or spray goes on the gum first, so you do not feel the needle. You are anaesthetised for the treatment itself, and afterwards you are given antibiotics and painkillers, so post-operative pain is very minimal.

A root canal is usually one to four visits, with each session around half an hour.
